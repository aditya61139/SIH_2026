## 💡 Pro Tips for Natural Delivery

1. **Don't read word-for-word** — internalize the key points and speak naturally
2. **Pause after the detection moment** — let the visual do the talking for 2–3 seconds before narrating
3. **Show genuine reaction** — a subtle "Whoa, look at that" or "There it is" when the gauge goes red feels authentic
4. **Vary your pace** — slow down for the hook and detection moment, speed up for tech specs
5. **Use your cursor as a pointer** — hover over key UI elements as you explain them
6. **Record in one take** — cuts in a 2-min video feel jarring
7. **End confidently** — the last line should feel like a mic drop
